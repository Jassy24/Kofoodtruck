Who we are

Our truck is more than a place to grab a meal. It's where we try out new dishes built on Kenny Oakley's Texas background.

Kenny has worked in restaurants since he was 12, starting as a bus boy at a Mexican restaurant in Amarillo, Texas. Today we bring that food to stops all over the Greater Charlotte area. Follow us on social media for our schedule and surprise visits.
