Who we are

Kenny's been cooking since he was twelve.

Our truck is more than a place to grab a meal. It's a place to try the new dishes Kenny Oakley creates from his Texas background.

Kenny has been in the restaurant business since age 12, starting as a bus boy at a local Mexican restaurant in Amarillo, Texas. His background runs through Tex-Mex, Italian and BBQ, and you'll taste all three on the truck.

We bring great food to locations all over the Greater Charlotte area and the counties around it. Follow us on Facebook and Instagram to see where we'll be next, and for surprise visits.
