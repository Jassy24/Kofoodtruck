Who we are

Our truck is more than a place to grab a meal. It is a place to try out our new dishes created by Kenny Oakley’s Texas background. Kenny has been in the restaurant industry since he was 12 years old beginning as a bus boy in a local Mexican restaurant in Amarillo, TX. His background includes Tex-Mex, Italian, and BBQ.

We bring great food to locations all over the Greater Charlotte area and can be found in the counties throughout the region. Our calendar below shows our scheduled stops but be sure to follow our social media for surprise visits!
